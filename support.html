Support

Need help with 85Blends, have feedback, or want to report an issue with the app or website? We’re here to help.

Contact support

Email us with your question, issue, or feedback. Please include helpful details like your app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were trying to do.

Support email:

support@85blends.app

Helpful details to include

  • What screen or feature you were using, such as Calculator, Stations, Garage, Fuel Log, or Reminders.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue.
  • Screenshots, if they help explain the problem.
  • Your vehicle setup details only if relevant to the issue.

Station or price reports

If you notice incorrect E85 station information or stale fuel price details, send the station name, address, and what needs to be updated.
