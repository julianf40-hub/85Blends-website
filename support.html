Common help topics

Why can’t I reach my target blend?

The fuel already in your tank and the pump percentages limit the range you can reach when filling it. The web calculator shows the reachable range. Recheck measured values rather than assuming every E85 pump contains 85% ethanol.

Why does a cheaper fill-up cost more to drive?

Higher ethanol blends can reduce MPG. The homepage comparison separates fill-up price from estimated cost per 100 miles, so you can compare the same driving distance.

Where are Pro subscriptions managed?

Pro is purchased through the App Store. You can manage or cancel your subscription through Settings on your iPhone. If your access doesn’t look right, email us with your app version and a description of the issue; never send payment details.
