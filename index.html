A few useful answers

Before your next fill-up.

How much E85 do I need for E30?

It depends on tank capacity, the fuel already in the tank, its ethanol percentage, and the actual pump fuel. Calculate your blend using measured values when available.

Can my vehicle run E85?

Only use ethanol concentrations approved for your vehicle or supported by its equipment and tune. Follow your vehicle manufacturer or tuner guidance; the calculator does not determine compatibility.

Is 85Blends free?

Yes. The core calculator, At The Pump, stations, fuel log, reminders, and one vehicle profile are included free. Pro adds advanced tools and an ad-free experience.

Where do station reports come from?

Station discovery and community prices help you find fuel. Reports can become outdated; always verify pump labels and station details before fueling.
