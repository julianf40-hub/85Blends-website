Account for tank capacity, current fuel, and actual pump ethanol content to calculate your target mix.

  • Custom ethanol targets
  • Gallons of high-ethanol fuel and gasoline
  • Saved vehicle defaults in the app

At The Pump

A plan for the fuel you’re adding.

Use the app’s partial-fill guidance when you are not filling the tank all the way.

  • Exact gallons for the planned fill
  • Partial-fill calculations
  • Blend warnings and pump guidance
Station finder

Find your next E85 stop.

Browse nearby stations, check community prices, and keep your favorite locations close.

  • Nearby map and station details
  • Community prices and station sharing
  • Saved stations for your regular stops
Trip Planner · Pro

Plan the drive, not just the fill.

Estimate range, plan fuel stops, and set an arrival reserve for your next road trip.

  • Estimated range on your blend
  • Fuel-stop recommendations
  • Arrival reserve and route risk indicators
Compare Free & Pro →
Vehicle garage

Your setup, ready next time.

Store tank capacity, ethanol targets, and vehicle details so every calculation starts with your setup.

  • One vehicle free; unlimited with Pro
  • Saved blend and tank defaults
  • Odometer and service tracking
Fuel log

See your fuel story over time.

Keep fill-ups, ethanol mix, prices, and mileage together to understand your real-world fuel use.

  • Gallons and blend history
  • Fuel cost and price per gallon
  • Mileage and MPG trends
Reminders

Keep maintenance in view.

Set mileage or date-based reminders for oil changes, spark plugs, or your own recurring tasks.

  • Mileage and date triggers
  • Custom labels and notes
  • Per-vehicle service tracking
