Blend Calculator

Hit your target ethanol — every fill-up.

Enter your tank size, current ethanol level, and target blend. 85Blends instantly calculates exactly how many gallons of E85 and regular gas to add.

  • Supports E20, E30, E50, E60, E85, or any custom target
  • Works with any flex-fuel tank size
  • Accounts for what's already in your tank
  • Instant results — no internet required

At The Pump

Already have fuel in the tank?
