Overview

85Blends helps E85 and flex-fuel drivers calculate ethanol blends, find fueling stations, save vehicle details, and track fill-ups. Availability of other features, such as reminders, trip planning, subscriptions, and cloud sync, depends on the platform and app version. The iOS app is available through the Apple App Store. The Android app is being developed for Google Play; its current development builds do not yet include advertising or Pro subscription purchases. This policy covers the services each version uses when those features are available.

85Blends is an independently operated app. We aim to limit information collection to what is needed for the app, community station information, support, and services you choose to use.

Information you enter in the app

The app may allow you to enter information such as vehicle nicknames, vehicle details, tank size, target ethanol blend, odometer readings, fuel logs, fuel prices, station names, notes, and reminder details. This information is used to provide app features such as blend calculations, garage defaults, fill-up history, and service reminders.

Local storage, iCloud, and Android backups

iOS: Vehicle profiles, fuel logs, reminders, preferences, and calculator data may be stored locally. iOS also supports syncing supported app data through Apple iCloud/CloudKit (for Free and Pro users), tied to the device's Apple ID and Apple account settings. Apple's privacy and data-retention practices govern its cloud service.

Android: The Android app stores Garage profiles, fuel logs, preferences, and other personal app information in on-device storage. It does not use iCloud and does not currently provide automatic cross-platform synchronization with the iOS app. Depending on Android/device backup settings, eligible local app data may also be included in an operating-system backup managed by the device's backup provider; clearing data on the device does not necessarily erase earlier backups.

Location information

iOS nearby stations: When you request nearby E85 stations, iOS uses your location and sends search coordinates to the Alternative Fuels Data Center (AFDC) station-location service to return nearby stations. You can change location permissions in iOS Settings.

iOS Automatic Pump Detection (optional): When enabled, this optional feature can use background/"Always" location permission to recognize arrival near fuel stations and help prompt a fill-up. It is off by default, requires a separate permission, and can be disabled in the app or iOS Settings.

Android nearby stations: The Android app requests a one-time foreground location fix when you choose "Use my location" in Stations. You may allow approximate or precise location, or decline and search for a place manually. For a nearby-station request, search coordinates are sent over HTTPS to the 85Blends Supabase station-catalogue proxy, which forwards the search to the AFDC data provider. The proxy is not designed to store your submitted search coordinates. Android does not currently request background location or provide Automatic Pump Detection.

Location information is used to respond to the requested location features, not to display your live location to other 85Blends users or sell it. A displayed station's location identifies the station, not your personal location. Service providers may have their own operational logging and retention practices.

Community pricing, ethanol reports, and station information

85Blends lets people contribute E85 station information, price reports, and ethanol-content reports ("Community Data"). A submission may include a station's name and address or station coordinates, reported fuel price or ethanol percentage, timestamp, app version, and, where supported, optional note or other report details. Both iOS and Android community-report services use Supabase to store and return Community Data.

The app also submits an app-installation-specific random reporter identifier with community reports to help rate-limit misuse, distinguish contributions, and maintain the reports. This identifier is not a login, your name, email address, or Apple ID. It is not used for cross-app advertising. Android's current reporting flow submits numeric price and ethanol readings without an account. Community station details and reports may be seen by other users; their accuracy and freshness are not guaranteed.

If you request removal of a report, we may ask for its station, approximate submission time, and reported value so we can locate it without exposing or deleting someone else's submission. See how to request data deletion.

Diagnostics and technical information

The app or website may receive limited technical information needed to operate, troubleshoot, protect, or improve the service. This may include device or browser type, app version, crash or error information, and general usage events. We do not use this information to sell your personal data.

Website information

When you visit 85Blends.app, standard web hosting systems may process basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, pages requested, and timestamps. This information is used for security, reliability, and site operation.

Third-party services

Services differ by platform and feature. These may include Apple iCloud/CloudKit and the App Store (iOS), Google's mapping and location services (Android), Supabase (community reports and Android station-catalogue proxy), the AFDC station-data provider, map and navigation services, RevenueCat (iOS subscription management), Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (advertising and consent in supported releases), email support, and website hosting. For example, Google Maps SDK may process device, map, and technical information under Google's own terms and privacy policies. These providers may process information necessary for the selected feature and may have independent retention and security practices.

Advertising and partner links

iOS: Supported iOS builds use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show advertisements, along with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent choices where required. Depending on Google settings and consent, Google and its partners may process device or advertising identifiers, technical information, and ad interactions for serving and measuring ads. Consult Google's privacy policies and the applicable App Store privacy disclosures.

Android: At the date of this policy, the Android development app does not include the AdMob advertising SDK or show network ads. If ads are introduced in a future Android release, we will update relevant disclosures before release.

The app and website may link to automotive partners or sponsors, including eFlexFuel and RVP Supply. Following a partner link takes you to that provider's website, governed by its own privacy policy. 85Blends does not sell your personal information. On iOS, 85Blends does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission or intentionally use IDFA for cross-app tracking.

Subscriptions and purchases

iOS: Available 85Blends Pro plans are billed through your Apple ID and the App Store. The iOS app uses RevenueCat to help validate and restore entitlements. Subscription services may process an app-specific identifier and purchase or transaction records, but 85Blends does not receive your card details from Apple. Manage, cancel, or restore purchases through the app and your Apple subscription settings.

Android: The Android development build does not currently offer paid Pro purchases. If Google Play subscriptions become available in a released Android version, we will update this section and the Google Play data disclosures to explain the payment and entitlement services in that build. Do not enter payment information in a support request.

Children's privacy

85Blends is an automotive utility intended primarily for adult drivers. The Android app's target audience is adults aged 18 and over. 85Blends is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us.

Your choices

  • Use the controls available in your app version to edit or remove vehicle information, fuel logs, and other local records.
  • Manage iOS location and optional Automatic Pump Detection in the app and iOS Settings. Android location can be denied or changed in Android Settings; Stations supports manual search.
  • Manage iOS iCloud/CloudKit through your Apple ID and device settings. Android system backups depend on the device and backup settings.
  • For supported ad-enabled app versions, manage privacy and consent choices through the available in-app or device settings.
  • Manage iOS Pro subscriptions through Apple's subscription settings.
  • Request assistance or deletion of eligible server-side Community Data at 85Blends Data Deletion.

Data retention and deletion

Personal vehicle details, fuel logs, and preferences saved locally remain until you change or remove them or erase the app's local storage, subject to operating-system backups and any iOS iCloud copies. Deleting local app data does not automatically delete community reports stored on Supabase. iCloud information may require separate action in Apple settings or assistance.

Community prices, ethanol reports, station records, and associated anonymous reporter identifiers may be stored in Supabase while needed to operate community features, correct reports, prevent misuse, and maintain station history. We do not currently apply a universal automatic 90-day deletion schedule or promise a fixed retention period for every report. Independently contributed station details and other users' reports may remain after your own report is removed. Technical, hosting, and support records may also be retained under provider policies or where legally required.

To request deletion, visit our Data Deletion page or email support@85blends.app. We will review sufficiently identifiable requests, request clarification if needed, and delete eligible records unless retention is necessary for legitimate operational, safety, or legal reasons. We will explain when a report cannot be located or certain records must be retained. We respond within applicable legal deadlines.

Data security

We use reasonable measures designed to protect information associated with the app and website. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the app or website after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Privacy or deletion request? See how to delete your 85Blends data or email support@85blends.app.
