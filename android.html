What's working in device builds

Built for everyday fueling.

These features have working Android foundations in the development app. They're still being refined and aren't promises of launch-day availability.

01 / Calculate

Blend calculator & Pump view

Calculate target ethanol blends, work through a fill-up, compare fuel cost assumptions, and save a fill-up to your local history.

02 / Discover

Nearby E85 stations

Find stations using optional foreground location or a manual place search. Browse a map or station list, see available community reports, and open directions.

03 / Contribute

Price & ethanol reports

Submit an E85 price or ethanol percentage for a station without creating an account. Community data is shared across our app platforms.

04 / Garage

Vehicle defaults

Keep vehicle details and tank information on your Android device and use the active vehicle in calculations.

05 / Fuel log

Fill-up history

View fill-ups saved from the Calculator, including details and vehicle filters. Further editing and reporting improvements are still in progress.

06 / More

Preferences & community partners

Adjust fuel calculation preferences and explore featured automotive partners from within the Android app.

iPhone and Android

One mission. Two native apps.

We're bringing practical 85Blends tools to Android while keeping the experience appropriate to each platform.

Android right now

  • Native Kotlin/Jetpack Compose development
  • Private on-device testing and reliability work
  • Shared E85 station and community reporting data
  • Garage and fill-up information saved locally
  • No Android Pro purchasing or in-app ads in current development builds

Still to come

  • Google Play closed testing and release preparation
  • Additional polish, accessibility and device testing
  • Evaluation of more iOS features for Android
  • Official Android screenshots captured from real app builds

Features, pricing, availability, and subscriptions may differ between iOS and Android. Current iOS Pro pricing does not imply Android subscriptions are available.

Android testing
