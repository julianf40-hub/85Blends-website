My Story

How 85Blends Started

JF
Julian F.
Founder, 85Blends · 2017 Dodge Charger R/T Scat Pack

I drive a 2017 Dodge Charger R/T Scat Pack and, after finally beginning to modify it, I decided to make the switch to E85 with a flex-fuel kit. The performance gains completely changed the car and showed me how capable ethanol blends can be.

As I learned more about E85, I found myself searching for stations, using multiple calculators, and relying on advice from my cousin and other enthusiasts. While there were some helpful tools available, none offered everything I personally wanted in one place.

That's what inspired me to create 85Blends.

I wanted one app dedicated to the entire E85 experience—helping drivers find nearby stations, calculate custom blends, track fuel usage, plan trips, set reminders, and make ethanol easier to understand and use.

One of the biggest reasons behind 85Blends is education. Many owners of flex-fuel vehicles don't even realize E85 is an option for them. My goal is to help introduce more enthusiasts to ethanol and make getting started less intimidating.

"I built the app I wished existed when I first started running E85."

Our Mission

Our Mission

We believe E85 should be easier to understand and easier to use.

Our mission is to provide enthusiasts with practical tools that help them confidently run ethanol blends, discover stations, learn more about flex-fuel options, and keep their vehicles performing at their best.

As the community grows, 85Blends will continue evolving with new features and ideas driven by real-world enthusiasts.

Community

Founding Supporter

85Blends would not exist without the support and encouragement of my cousin, whose own automotive journey helped inspire mine.

As I began learning more about E85 and the world of performance modifications, he was one of the people who encouraged me to keep exploring, keep learning, and eventually turn the idea for 85Blends into a real app.

Through RVPSupply, he has built a business centered around supporting automotive enthusiasts and helping people pursue their own projects and passions.

Today, RVPSupply proudly supports 85Blends as a founding supporter and sponsor. Their encouragement and early belief in this project helped transform an idea into a real app serving the E85 community.

The people who encourage our journeys often help shape where they lead.
